P05 Urban Accessibility — Step 5 Accessibility Gaps
=========================================================

Operational definition:
Potential accessibility gap = pedestrian-network node more than 1500 m
from the nearest pharmacy along the walking network.

≤ 500 m: 19950 nodes (35.78%)
500–1000 m: 22559 nodes (40.46%)
1000–1500 m: 7835 nodes (14.05%)
> 1500 m: 5406 nodes (9.70%)

Potential gap nodes (>1500 m): 5406
Potential gap share: 9.70%

Interpretation note:
The 1500 m threshold is used here as an operational screening threshold, not as a universal standard of adequate pharmacy access.
